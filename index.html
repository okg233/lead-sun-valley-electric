About Sun Valley Electric

Kelowna & Okanagan's Most Prominent Electrical Contractor.

Sun Valley Electric is a local company started by Tony Mcarthy in 1989. He built the company with a vision to provide high quality workmanship and professional service to Kelowna and the surrounding area. Now owned and run by Isaac Rowland, this vision is still at the heart of all operations. Almost three decades later and Sun Valley Electric is now one of the most prominent electric companies in the Okanagan.

Our motto has always been "We bring good things to life." Whether that be your luxury custom home, a large apartment complex, repairing a malfunctioning component, or bringing up the next generation of competent electrical apprentices.

After 3 decades of serving the Kelowna and Okanagan region, Sun Valley Electric continues to deliver and expand on the same roots of quality and commitment in every project we undertake, big or small. With an extensive fleet and team of journeymen, we have the knowledge and experience to tackle any scale of project.
